    Your mission

    • Collect fish, seaweed and shells for +10, golden shells for +50 and cleanup tokens for +100.
    • Jump over floating plastic. Fishing nets are too tall — switch lanes to avoid them.
    • A bubble shield protects one heart.
    • Hit plastic and the game pauses so you can learn a real fact and take a 2-question quiz. Then keep swimming!

    About the Mission

    Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic enter our oceans, threatening sea turtles, fish, seabirds and countless other marine species.

    Ocean Guardian turns an endless-runner adventure into an awareness experience: every pollution encounter becomes a learning moment, not just an obstacle.

    Play, learn, and carry a little more ocean-awareness with you after every swim.
